# 贡献者公约 | Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> 参与本项目即表示您同意遵守本公约。Violations may be reported to **admin@0379.email**.

---

## 承诺 | Our Pledge

我们作为成员、贡献者和维护者承诺：让本社区的参与体验对所有人都是无骚扰的——无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人形象、种族、宗教或性取向。

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺以有助于建设开放、友好、多元、包容、健康社区的方式行事与互动。

---

## 准则 | Our Standards

**鼓励的行为 | Examples of positive behavior**

- 对他人展现同理心与善意 | Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- 尊重不同意见、观点与经验 | Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- 给予并优雅接受建设性反馈 | Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 承担责任、向受影响者致歉并从中学习 | Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes
- 以社区利益为先，而非仅顾个人 | Focusing on what is best for the overall community

**不可接受的行为 | Unacceptable behavior**

- 性化语言/图像及任何形式的骚扰 | Sexualized language/imagery and harassment of any kind
- 恶意挑衅、侮辱、贬损性评论或人身/政治攻击 | Trolling, insulting comments, personal or political attacks
- 公开或私下骚扰 | Public or private harassment
- 未经明确许可发布他人的私人信息 | Publishing others' private information without permission
- 其它在专业场合可合理认定不当的行为 | Other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## 职责 | Enforcement Responsibilities

社区维护者有责任解释并执行本公约，对可接受的行为做出公平、适当的纠正行动，并对任何不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取澄清与处置措施（包括警告、临时或永久封禁）。

维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝与本公约不符的评论、提交、代码、Issue 及其他贡献。

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair, appropriate corrective action, including warnings or temporary/permanent bans. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code.

---

## 范围 | Scope

本公约适用于所有社区空间（仓库、Issue、PR、Discussions、聊天群、线下活动），也适用于个人在公共场合正式代表本项目时的行为。

This Code applies within all community spaces (repo, issues, PRs, discussions, chat, events) and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

---

## 执行 | Enforcement

违例行为请通过 **admin@0379.email** 举报。所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审查与调查，并尊重事件各方的隐私与安全。

Report violations to **admin@0379.email**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and security of the reporter. Enforcement outcomes will be published (redacted) when appropriate.

| 处置级别 Level | 适用情形 Scenario | 措施 Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 纠正 Correction | 不当措辞或轻微越界 | 私下书面警告 + 说明原因 |
| 2 · 警告 Warning | 单次或系列违规 | 限期整改，期间禁止互动 |
| 3 · 临时封禁 Temporary ban | 严重或持续违规 | 指定期限内禁止一切社区互动 |
| 4 · 永久封禁 Permanent ban | 持续违规/骚扰/攻击特定群体 | 永久禁止参与 |
